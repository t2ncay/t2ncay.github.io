I am a Computer Engineering undergraduate at Türkiye-Azerbaijan University. I currently serve as a Teaching Assistant for the Computer Laboratory.

My research interests lie in **compiler design**, **deterministic GPU execution**, and **memory-safe systems for Edge AI**. I am particularly interested in region-based memory management and its applications in deploying neural networks to bare-metal microcontrollers.

I am the creator of **Vyne**, a systems programming language with a multi-target backend (x86, CUDA PTX, AVR, ESP32) that enforces deterministic training and strict compile-time memory bounds (`@bounded`).

Feel free to reach out via email if you'd like to discuss compilers, CUDA, or hardware.
